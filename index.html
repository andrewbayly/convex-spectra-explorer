Convex spectra: dissected regular polygons

An interactive companion to the one-pager by Yoshiaki Araki and Teruhisa Sugimoto (April 2021), answering Erich Friedman’s Convex Spectra problem. One piece, many convex arrangements. Pick the piece with k, then grow (r) and stretch (s) the arrangement. Source on GitHub.

j: slices per crescent (j = 1 is “Type 3-double”)
